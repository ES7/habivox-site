Get started

Know what you can spend today.

HabiVox turns your income into a budget for each day, keeps rent and bills apart, and its AI coach shows where your money quietly goes. Your habits live in the same app.

Daily budget

A salary is a monthly number. Spending happens daily.

Set your income, rent, EMI and what you want to save. HabiVox works out what you can spend each day.

  • Today's budget

    See what's left for today as you log.

  • Rent stays apart

    Rent, EMI and bills never count against a day.

  • Big buys spread out

    A phone or a flight is spread over the rest of the month.

  • Saved this month

    Money you didn't spend is saving, not extra to spend.

  • Daily vs budget

    Every day of the month, within budget or over.

  • Morning and night

    Today's budget in the morning, the day's result at night. Android app.

AI Coach

A coach that reads your month, not just your receipts.

Once a week the coach looks at your spending and your habits, tells you what matters, and gives you one thing to change.

  • Coach's verdict

    Two honest lines on how your month is going.

  • Money health score

    Out of 100: saving, wants, daily control and logging.

  • Month-end forecast

    How much you'll save at your current pace.

  • Small spends that add up

    Coffee, food delivery, quick commerce, shown per year.

  • One change, one challenge

    The change that saves the most, and a weekly limit to try.

  • A plan for your habits

    One small task for every day of the week.

Included free. No API key or setup needed.

Spending insights

Know where the money went, and what you kept.

Add income and expenses in a few taps. HabiVox sorts them and compares them for you.

  • Categories

    A total for food, travel and the rest.

  • Vs last month

    The same days compared, so it's fair.

  • Saved this year

    And since the first income you logged.

  • Quick add

    One tap repeats a recent expense.

Habits

Track each habit the way you measure it.

Yes or no, a count, litres, hours of sleep or a timer. Quit habits show the money you save.

  • Yes or no, count, amount

    Steps, litres, glasses or your own unit.

  • Duration

    Start a timer, or type the hours and minutes.

  • Quit habits

    Set a daily maximum and a cost, and see the money you save.

  • Calendar view

    Each day shaded by how much you got done.

  • Past entries

    Forgot yesterday? Log it later.

  • Reminders

    Pick a reminder time for each habit.
